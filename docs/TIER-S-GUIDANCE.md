# runyth-tables-of-sustenance-1.1.1

## Tier S Guidance

Tier S foods remain legendary narrative rewards. Ordinary random generation excludes direct Tier S foods unless the GM explicitly enables legendary generation.

The core permanently records each consumed benefit by stable `effectId`. Duplicate consumption is rejected before serving reduction. Removing a benefit in Benefit Manager delegates removal of its mechanical representation to the active system adapter and then clears the core record.

A campaign may encounter a Tier S reward roughly once in many dozens of sessions, or never. They are not intended as routine progression, shopping inventory or crafting output.
